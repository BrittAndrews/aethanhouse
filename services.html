Mix And Match

Pick a tier per room, not per house.

Rooms are not equal, and pricing them as if they were is how people end up paying for drawings they did not need. Put the attention where you want it.

For example

  • Kitchen — Tier 3, because the cabinets get built
  • Primary bath — Tier 2, specified and shoppable
  • Guest room — Tier 1, direction and what to keep
  • Hallway — left alone this year

Whole-home ranges assume a mix like that one. Rooms can also be added later at your package's per-room rate — nothing about the first document expires.

Build your own mix on the contact page. It gives you a rough range before you get in touch, and final pricing is agreed before payment is expected.

Common Questions

Answered before you ask.

Do you do the buying?

No. You buy, at your own pace, with the list. Nothing is marked up because nothing passes through me.

Do you work outside Atlanta?

Yes. Out-of-town projects run the Design Direction Session as a remote review.

Can I start with one room?

Most people do. Single rooms are priced per room at every tier, and rooms can be added later.

What if I already have a contractor?

Better. Tier 3 includes the renovation details to hand off to your GC, and I answer their questions directly.
