Case Study · Atlanta, GA · Specified

The Delaney nursery, built wall to wall.

A ten-by-twelve room with one usable wall. Instead of buying four pieces of furniture that would not fit it, we drew one piece that does: closet, changing table, books and toys in a single run of millwork, with the mural kept visible between the openings.

Scope
Nursery · millwork elevations and finishes
Tier
Tier 3 · design development
Status
Drawn and specified · with the cabinet shop

Concept

The mural is the wall, so the cabinets had to step back.

Every opening in the millwork is sized to a piece of the mural, so the room keeps its animals.

The elevation was drawn against the mural panel layout, not against a standard cabinet grid. The giraffe, the elephant and the lion each land in an open bay rather than behind a door.

Cabinet fronts are painted the mural's own background tone, which is why a wall of storage does not read as a wall of storage.

What was specified

The sheet the cabinet shop quoted from.

  • Millwork — inset Shaker, painted warm cream, satin
  • Bays — five open, sized to the mural panel repeat
  • Changing surface — removable tray on the drawer bank
  • Hardware — unlacquered brass pulls, no knobs below 40 inches
  • Mural — pre-pasted panels, hung before the millwork
  • Lighting — dimmable ceiling fixture, no downlight over the crib
