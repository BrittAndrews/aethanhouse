The Practice

Hi, I'm Britt.

Aethan House is one person, working out of Atlanta on remodels, restorations and new builds. You talk to the person who draws the plan, and the plan is written clearly enough that whoever builds it can keep going without me on the phone.

I pay attention to how people actually live in a room — where the keys land, which chair nobody sits in, what the mornings look like. A house that works with those habits is one you stop fighting.

So I write down how your home should look, room by room, and hand you the document. You take it from there — with your builder, your contractor, or on your own weekends. There is no wrong pace.
